Engineering Troubleshooting & Lessons Learned

Field-Tested Problem Resolutions

Challenge Encountered Root Cause Engineering Resolution
HTTPS browsing failed despite ssl/web-browsing being allowed. PAN-OS parsed TLS Client Hello SNI and promoted sessions mid-flow to google-base, which wasn't in the rule. Grouped interdependent cloud apps in Standard-Internet-Apps while excluding streaming apps (youtube-base).
GlobalProtect & Inbound DMZ HTTPS conflicted on WAN TCP 443. Both GlobalProtect Gateway and DMZ Web Server required listening on port 443 on ethernet1/1. Bound secondary VIP 192.168.1.201/32 to ethernet1/1, isolating GlobalProtect on .200 and DMZ NAT on .201.
Inbound DNAT & GlobalProtect sessions timed out on return. Internal hosts lacked reverse routes for remote GP pools (10.200.1.0/24) or external IPs. Paired DNAT / GP ingress rules with Source NAT (PAT) translating to the firewall LAN gateway (10.10.10.1).
PBF failover blackholed traffic after Tunnel1 shutdown. Once PBF disabled the rule on 3 missed probes, the VR lacked an alternate route to 20.20.20.0/24. Added floating static route CSR-BACKUP (AD 20) via ethernet1/1, shifting traffic cleanly to the underlay.
